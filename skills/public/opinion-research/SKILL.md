# Public Opinion Research

Confirm the topic, time window, geography, platforms, and intended audience from the request. State any assumptions that affect the findings.

Use evidence supplied by the user or tools actually available in DeerFlow. Record source URLs or identifiers, publication times when available, and relevant excerpts. Separate observed facts, opinions, and your interpretation; describe sampling gaps and conflicting evidence.

Return a concise evidence summary and open questions. Phase 0 provides guidance only: automated social collection, retrieval pipelines, sentiment scoring, and the legacy research graph have not been migrated. Do not claim to have run them or invent counts, coverage, or citations.
