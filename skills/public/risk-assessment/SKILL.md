# Public Opinion Risk Assessment

Identify the affected stakeholders and the time window. For each risk, cite the supporting evidence, describe the plausible impact, and state what remains unknown. Distinguish observed developments from scenarios.

Use qualitative judgments with an explicit rationale. Do not invent probability estimates, numerical risk scores, sentiment metrics, or platform-wide prevalence from an unrepresentative sample.

Suggest proportionate follow-up research or human review. Phase 0 has no migrated automated risk engine, monitoring service, or alert delivery; report those capabilities as unavailable when requested.
